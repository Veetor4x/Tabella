# Tabella logo concepts (comparison only, nothing applied)

Direction: heavy cut-up type with one small wax-red detail. Font: Poppins Black (Poppins Project Authors), SIL Open Font License 1.1,
free for commercial use. Letters are converted to outlines and cut with real boolean operations in `tools/logo-concepts.py`;
`tools/logo-sheet.cjs` builds `tabella-logo-concepts.png`. SVGs here are paths: pearl, night, and bare (`-mark`) versions, plus app icons.

- A, the Seam: one horizontal slice through every letter, halves offset, the seal in the seam (uppercase and lowercase)
- B, Stencil: stencil bridges through T, A, B, E, L; wax fills one crack in the B
- C, Stacked block: TAB over ELLA, two cuts through both rows, a red square closing one cut
